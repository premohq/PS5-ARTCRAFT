01 · The suite

Thirteen apps. One desktop.

Every app ArtCraft Launcher's catalog lists, installed the way the launcher installs them, so it adopts them and keeps them up to date on the console as it does on a PC. The versions below are the ones this edition pins.

02 · Your files In development

Your files, wherever they live.

ArtCraft Files runs on the PS5's desktop and connects the apps to the servers you already have.

Server drives

Open and save on your home server

A shared folder on your PC or NAS (SMB), Nextcloud or any WebDAV server, or an FTP server shows up as a drive, H:, I: and on, in every app's Open and Save windows. Changes go both ways.

Send to the PS5

Drop files in over FTP

Point any FTP app on your PC or phone at the PS5 and copy pictures, footage and projects straight into Documents, Pictures, Videos or Music. No USB drive.

Backups

Keep a copy of your work

Your work is copied to your home server or an FTP server when you ask, and as you save. Nothing on the server is deleted by a backup.

03 · On the couch

A PC's creative studio, on the TV.

USB keyboard and mouse

Plug them into the PS5 and work the way the apps were made to be used.

A desktop of its own

ArtCraft starts straight into its own desktop, with a file manager, Settings and a shortcut for every app.

Updates itself

ArtCraft Launcher starts with the desktop and keeps itself and every app up to date.

Official releases

Each app is the ArtCraft Team's own Windows release, pinned by version and checked by SHA-256.

04 · How it works

Three steps from your PC.

  1. 1

    Build it

    On a Linux PC (or WSL2), fetch and check every pinned release and lay out what the console gets.

    python3 -m artcraft_ps5 build
  2. 2

    Send it

    Copy it to a jailbroken PS5 over FTP. Later runs send only what changed.

    python3 -m artcraft_ps5 deploy
  3. 3

    Start ArtCraft

    The ArtCraft title opens its desktop, and ArtCraft Launcher starts on it.

    PS5 → ArtCraft
